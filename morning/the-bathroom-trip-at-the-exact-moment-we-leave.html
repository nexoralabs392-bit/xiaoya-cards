For the Bathroom Trip at the Exact Moment We Leave

Four lines you can say out loud tonight. Word for word. Free to save.

What to say

  1. The last bathroom stop is before shoes, not after.

  2. It shows up at the exact moment we’re late, and that timing is rotten luck.

  3. Two minutes in the bathroom, and I wait by the door with the bag on my shoulder.

  4. Either way I wait those two minutes and then we leave. The car is already running.

When to use it

This is for the moment it actually goes wrong — not the calm version you rehearse in the car. Pick one line. Say it once. Then stop talking and wait three seconds. If they push back, repeat the same line, not a new one. Say the same thing for five nights before you decide it doesn't work.
