For the Child Who Says It Is Too Much

Four lines you can say out loud tonight. Word for word. Free to save.

What to say

  1. Cleanup happens before dinner, and crying is allowed while we do it.

  2. A full floor looks impossible. That feeling is real and it doesn’t mean you can’t.

  3. I put four toys in the basket and you put in four, taking turns until the rug is clear.

  4. If it gets too big, you say stop and we sit on the floor for one minute.

When to use it

This is for the moment it actually goes wrong — not the calm version you rehearse in the car. Pick one line. Say it once. Then stop talking and wait three seconds. If they push back, repeat the same line, not a new one. Say the same thing for five nights before you decide it doesn't work.
